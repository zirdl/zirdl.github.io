    • Alipato at Muog, Screened in La Union

    Alipato at Muog, Directed by JL Burgos, brother to Jonas Burgos, an enforced disappearance victim, and whom the documentary is about, was screened in various schools and a Library in La Union. Alipato at Muog was awarded the Special Jury Award during the Cinemalaya Film Festival of 2024 and was rated X by the MTRCB, which then was lifted as there’s no substantial basis for the rating.
